SECTION: 1. CMEGP Overview and Eligibility
Chief Minister Employment Generation Programme (CMEGP) is a state-level credit-linked subsidy scheme launched by the Government of Maharashtra to promote micro-enterprises across manufacturing and service sectors. Permanent residents of Maharashtra (Domicile Certificate required) aged between 18 and 45 years (up to 50 years for SC/ST/Women/Ex-Servicemen) are eligible.

SECTION: 2. Financial Limits and Subsidy Rates
Maximum project cost for manufacturing sector is Rs. 50 Lakhs and for service sector is Rs. 10 Lakhs. Margin money subsidy for rural projects is 25% for general category and 35% for special categories (SC/ST/OBC/Women/Physically Handicapped). For urban projects, subsidy rate is 15% for general category and 25% for special categories. Applicant contribution is 10% for general category and 5% for special category.
